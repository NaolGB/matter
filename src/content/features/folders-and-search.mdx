## The list of notes

Folders come first, in order of name, with how many notes each holds. Notes in no folder sit under **Notes**. Notes are ordered by their last edit, newest first, and each shows its emoji, title and first words.

A note with no title shows as **Untitled**. [The Pin board](/features/pin-board) is kept apart and never appears in this list.

### On the Mac

The list is in the sidebar on the Notes page. Coming back to the page reopens the note you were last reading.

### On the iPhone

With four or more notes, **Recent** rides on top: up to six cards for the notes you edited last. They are shortcuts to the same notes as the rows below. A note with no title and nothing written gets no card.

### Pick an emoji

Every note and folder can wear an emoji from Matter's set, and **Remove icon** takes it off.

- **On the Mac**, click the emoji beside a folder's name, or beside the title of the open note.
- **On the iPhone**, hold a note or folder and choose **Icon**, or tap the emoji at the top of an open note.

## Folders

Folders hold notes, and they don't nest.

**Make one.** On the Mac, click **New folder** in the toolbar or press **⌃⌘N**, then type the name in place. On the iPhone, open the circle beside the tab bar and tap **New folder**. A folder with no name shows as **Untitled folder**.

**Open one.** Click or tap its row to show its notes, and again to hide them.

**Rename one.** Right-click it on the Mac or hold it on the iPhone, and choose **Rename**.

**File a note.**

- **On the Mac**, drag it onto a folder, or right-click it and choose **Move to folder**. To take it out, drag it onto the **Notes** heading or choose **Remove from folder**.
- **On the iPhone**, hold it and choose **Move to folder** or **Remove from folder**.

## Where a new note goes

On the Mac, click **New note** in the toolbar or press **⌘N**. On the iPhone, open the circle beside the tab bar and tap **New note**, which is there while a note is open too. The new note opens straight away, filed like this:

1. **A folder you have opened** since you last picked a note takes it. Making a new folder counts as opening it, and closing the folder undoes this.
2. **Otherwise it goes beside the note you were in.** On the Mac that is the note you are reading. On the iPhone it is the note you last opened.
3. **If that note is in no folder,** or there is none, the new note is in no folder too.

**New note in folder**, on a folder's right-click or hold menu, always files there.

## Search the list

Quick find keeps the notes whose title or text holds your words, ignoring case. It reads the [mentions](/features/mentions) in a note too. Only folders whose name matches, or that hold a match, stay on show.

- **On the Mac**, use **Quick find notes…** in the toolbar, or press **⌥⌘F** to go to it. A closed folder stays closed while you search. Its count shows how many notes in it match, and a click opens it.
- **On the iPhone**, open the circle beside the tab bar and tap **Search**. Folders with a match open by themselves, so the matches are in view. **Recent** hides while you search.

## Find in a note

Find marks every match in the open note in yellow, with the current one brighter. Case and accents don't matter, and a match can't run across two lines. Find reads a note's lines. It doesn't read the title, or inside a [mention](/features/mentions).

### On the Mac

Press **⌘F**, or choose **Find…** under **Find** in the **Edit** menu. The toolbar's search field becomes **Find in note**, with your place beside it, such as **2 of 5**, or **No matches**.

- **Return** or **⌘G** goes to the next match. It carries on round from the last match to the first.
- **⇧Return** or **⇧⌘G** goes to the one before.
- **The arrows** beside the field go back and forward too.
- **⌘E** finds the words you have selected in the note. It is **Use selection for find** in the menu.
- **Esc** closes it and leaves the current match selected.

### On the iPhone

With a note open, open the circle beside the tab bar and tap **Find in note**. The system's find bar comes up over the keyboard. By default case and accents don't matter, and the bar's own options can change that. It finds only, and doesn't replace.

## Delete

Every delete asks first.

**A note** goes for good, with everything in it. There is no Archive for notes and no undo.

- **On the Mac**, right-click it and choose **Delete**, or press **⇧⌘⌫** for the open note. That key is **Delete note** in the **File** menu.
- **On the iPhone**, swipe the row to the left and tap **Delete**, or hold it and choose **Delete**.

**A folder** goes on its own, and its notes move out to **Notes**. Right-click it on the Mac or hold it on the iPhone, and choose **Delete folder**. The question says how many notes move, and that no note is deleted.

To delete lines inside a note, see [Select lines](/features/blocks#select-lines).

### Mac keyboard shortcuts

- **⌘N** New note
- **⌃⌘N** New folder
- **⌥⌘F** Quick find, for the list
- **⌘F** Find in the open note
- **⌘G** and **⇧⌘G** Next and previous match
- **⌘E** Use selection for find
- **⇧⌘⌫** Delete note
