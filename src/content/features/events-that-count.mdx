## Turn it on or off

- **On the Mac**, open **Settings ▸ Calendars** and use the **Treat as tasks** checkbox on the feed's row.
- **On the iPhone**, open **Settings ▸ Calendars** and use the **Treat as tasks** switch under the feed.

A new feed starts with it on. It applies to all of a feed's events at once, and can't be set for one event. A change shows straight away and syncs to your other device.

## With it on

- **Events sit on Tasks**, in Today and Upcoming, among your work. See [Task list with calendars](/features/tasks-with-calendars).
- **A timed event counts as planned work**, as a task with that estimate would. Its length goes into the day's planned time, so there is that much less free.
- **Time you track on it comes off what it still counts for**, and shows under **Worked** on the Mac.
- **Completing it takes it out of planned time.** Until you do, it counts for the whole day, even after it has ended. A 9:00 meeting you haven't ticked still counts at 15:00.
- **All of its length counts**, including any part outside your day's hours.
- **An event across several days** gives each day only the part that falls on it.
- **[Analysis](/features/analysis)** includes it in the week's planned time.

## With it off

- **Events show on the Calendar page only.** They leave Today and Upcoming, and the iPhone's Tasks card and the Mac's menu bar panel won't name one as next.
- **They don't count as planned work**, so they add nothing to the day's planned time.
- **They still take room from the day.** The part of a timed event inside your day's hours, and still ahead of now, comes off the room left. A day's budget is the smaller of your focus cap and that room, so this only shows when the room falls below the cap.
- **Analysis** leaves them out of the week's planned time. Time you track on one still counts as tracked.

## With it on or off

- **Matter places tasks around timed events.** Automatic placement puts a task on top of one only when no free stretch can hold it. See [Auto-placement](/features/auto-placement).
- **Events show on the Calendar page**, in their feed's colour.
- **Reminders**, if you have them on, come for timed events. See [Reminders](/features/reminders).
- **You can time them** with a [session](/features/sessions), from the event's sheet.
- **All-day events take no time.** They don't count as planned work, don't take room and don't move tasks. An event with no end time is the same.

## An example

Take a coming day with your day's hours at 8:00 to 20:00, a 6h focus cap, and nothing else planned.

- **A 2h event, on:** 2h planned against a budget of 6h, so 4h free.
- **A 2h event, off:** 10h of room leaves the budget at 6h, so 6h free.
- **A 7h event, off:** 5h of room brings the budget down to 5h, so 5h free.
