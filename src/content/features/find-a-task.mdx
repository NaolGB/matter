## Search the board

### On the Mac

Click **Quick find tasks…** at the top of Tasks, or press **⌘F**. Both columns narrow as you type. Press **⎋** or click the **✕** to clear it and bring everything back.

### On the iPhone

Tap the circle beside the tab bar, then **Search**. Tasks narrows as you type.

Coming days stay folded while you search. A day still listed under Upcoming holds a match, so tap it to see the match.

## What it finds

- **Open tasks** in Today and Upcoming, however far ahead.
- **Tasks you finished today**, in the Done group.
- **Events on your board**, from calendars set to [Treat as tasks](/features/events-that-count).
- **Coming tasks** from your [templates](/features/recurring), for the next seven days.

Work that is running isn't searched. It stays in the work bucket whatever you type.

## What it doesn't find

- **Tags, days or estimates.** Only the title counts.
- **Finished tasks from earlier days, and deleted tasks.** Search the [Archive](/features/complete-reopen-and-push) instead, which finds them by title the same way.

## Jump by tag on the iPhone

The rail down the right edge of Tasks has a mark for each group on the board: the top, each tag or calendar, **Done** and Upcoming. Tap a mark to jump to that group, or slide your finger along the rail, and the group's name and hours show beside your finger. The rail needs at least three marks to appear, and it steps aside while you search.

### Mac keyboard shortcuts

- **⌘F** goes to quick find on Tasks.
- **⎋** clears it.
