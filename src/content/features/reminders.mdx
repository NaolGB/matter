## What you get

A reminder is a notification with a sound. It has the event's title and how soon it starts, then the start time and the place if the event has one.

- **Events with a time** from any of your calendars get them, whether or not the calendar is set to [Treat as tasks](/features/events-that-count).
- **All-day events** don't.
- **Events you have marked done** don't.
- **Tasks** don't, even a task pinned to a time.

## Turn them on

### On the Mac

Open **Settings ▸ General** and find **Reminders** in the **App** group. It says **On**, or **Off** with a **Turn on** button.

macOS only asks once. If you said no before, **Turn on** opens System Settings, where you can allow notifications for Matter.

### On the iPhone

Matter asks the first time there is an event to remind you about. You can also tap the circle beside the tab bar, then **More** and **Settings**, and tap **Turn on reminders** under **General**.

If you said no, the row says **Off**, with **Open iOS Settings** under it.

Neither app has its own switch to turn reminders off once they are on. Use the system's notification settings.

## When they arrive

### On the Mac

Reminders only come while Matter is open. Matter checks each minute for anything due, and sends a reminder only within a minute of its mark. If you open Matter 20 minutes before an event, you get the 5 and 2 minute reminders, and not a late 30 minute one.

### On the iPhone

Reminders come whether or not Matter is open, because they are handed to iOS up to a week ahead. Matter updates them when it opens, when it comes back, and after each calendar refresh, including refreshes iOS allows in the background.

iOS holds only 64 waiting notifications per app, so Matter hands over the soonest 64. On a busy calendar that can cover only the next few days, and the later events are added as those go by.

An event changed in your calendar reaches the iPhone's reminders at its next refresh.

## With both apps

The Mac and the iPhone each remind you on their own. With Matter open on your Mac and reminders on for your iPhone, you get both.
