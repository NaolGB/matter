## What tags do

- **Group Today.** Today's tasks are listed under their tags, each group with the tag's symbol, name and total time. Upcoming is grouped by day instead.
- **Break down your time.** [Analysis](/features/analysis) shows where your time went by tag.
- **Teach Matter.** [Estimate suggestions](/features/estimates) and the [usual hour for a kind of work](/features/auto-placement) are both worked out per tag, and tasks with the same tag are placed together.

A task with two tags is listed under both. It is one task, so finishing it or starting a session from either place moves both.

A task with no tags goes under **No tag**. A calendar event with no tags goes under its calendar's name.

## Tags and tag types

Every tag sits in a tag type, which is a heading such as Clients, with tags such as each client's name under it. The iPhone calls a tag type a group.

A type can't have two tags with the same name, ignoring capitals and accents. Two different types can.

## Make tags

### On the Mac

Click **Tags** at the top of Tasks.

1. Click **+** for a **New tag type**, type its name and press **Return**.
2. The field for its first tag opens. Type a name and press **Return**. The field stays open for the next one.
3. Press **⎋** or leave the field empty when you are done.

Use **New tag** under a type to add to it later.

### On the iPhone

Tap the circle beside the tab bar, then **More**, **Settings** and **Tags**.

1. Type in the **New group** field and tap **Add**.
2. Tap **New tag** in that group, type a name and tap **Add**. The field stays open for the next one.

### While you are tagging

You don't have to leave the task. In the tag menu, type a name, and **Create “name” in** lists each type that doesn't have it yet. Pick one. If nothing matches, **Return** makes the tag in the first type listed.

A new type still has to be made in the Tags list.

## Tag a task

Open the task's [editor](/features/add-task) and open its tags, **Add tag** on the Mac and the **Tags** row on the iPhone. The menu stays open so you can pick several, and picking one again takes it off.

You can tag a calendar event from its sheet. The tag stays in Matter and is never written back to your calendar.

## Change tags

### On the Mac

In the **Tags** panel:

- **Rename.** Double-click the name, or right-click and choose **Rename**. This works for a type too.
- **Change the symbol.** Click the symbol, or right-click and choose **Change symbol**.
- **Hide.** Hover over the row and click the eye, or right-click and choose **Hide**.
- **Move or reorder.** Drag the tag, or right-click and choose **Move to**.
- **Delete.** Hover over the row and click the trash, or right-click and choose **Delete**.

### On the iPhone

In **Settings ▸ Tags**:

- **Rename, change the symbol, or move to another group.** Tap the tag. The sheet has **Name**, **Group** and **Symbol**.
- **Hide or delete.** Swipe the tag left, or tap it and use **Hidden** or **Delete tag**.
- **Reorder.** Tap **Edit** and drag the handles.
- **Rename or delete a group.** Tap its heading.

Renaming changes the tag everywhere it is used. A tag can't move to a type that already has one with its name.

## Symbols

Tags have symbols, not colours. A new tag gets a symbol at random from those no other tag has. In the picker, a symbol another tag has is faded and can't be picked, because two tags with one symbol would read as one.

## Hide or delete

- **Hide** when you have finished with a tag. It stays on everything that has it, but it is no longer offered when you tag new work. A hidden tag stays in the list, dimmed, so you can show it again.
- **Delete** when you want it gone. It comes off everything that has it, and the tasks themselves stay. Matter asks first.

Deleting a type deletes its tags too.
