## Set it

The same settings apply to both apps and sync between them. They apply to every day, so there is no separate shape for a weekend.

### On the Mac

Open **Settings ▸ General** with **⌘,** and use the **Day** group: **Start of day**, **End of day**, **Focus cap** and **Refuse work that doesn't fit**.

You can also change the hours from Calendar. Click the time range at the top of the page, such as **08:00 – 18:30**. It is the same setting.

### On the iPhone

Tap the circle beside the tab bar, then **More**, then **Settings**, and use the **Day** group: **Start**, **End**, **Focus cap** and **Refuse work that doesn't fit today**.

## What each one does

- **Start and end of day.** 8:00 to 20:00 unless you change them. Matter places tasks only inside these hours, and Calendar shades the rest. They stay at least 15 minutes apart. Loafy's day runs on them too.
- **Focus cap.** The most planned work one day holds, 6h unless you change it. The Mac offers 1h to 12h, and the iPhone steps from 30m to 12h in half hours.
- **Refuse work that doesn't fit.** Whether a full today stops you saving or only tells you. See [Avoid overplanning](/features/avoid-overplanning).

## How they make a budget

A day's budget is the smaller of your focus cap and the room the day has. Room is the time inside your hours, less any events from calendars you haven't set to [Treat as tasks](/features/events-that-count). All-day events take no room.

On today, room is measured from now, because the morning you have already had is gone. On a coming day it is the whole window.

With a day from 8:00 to 18:30, a 6h cap and an empty calendar:

- **At 9:41**, the room is 8h 49m, so the budget is the cap, 6h.
- **At 15:00**, the room is 3h 30m, so the budget is 3h 30m.
- **With 5h planned at 15:00**, the day reads **1h 30m over**.

Planned work is your open tasks, events from calendars set to **Treat as tasks**, and what your templates will add. See [Upcoming capacity](/features/upcoming-capacity).

## When you change it

The day's reading changes straight away. Tasks already placed stay where they are until the day is next [laid out](/features/auto-placement).

A task pinned outside your hours stays there and still counts toward the day.
