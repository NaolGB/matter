## When a task doesn't fit today

A task fits when its estimate is no more than what today has free. How much is free comes from your [day's shape](/features/day-shape) and what is already planned.

When it is more, the editor says **Doesn't fit today** under the day, with how much is left. Nothing else on the day moves to make room. You have three ways out:

- **Move it.** The **Move to** button names the soonest day with room, such as **Move to Sat, Nov 14**. It changes the day in the editor, and you still press **Done** or **Save**.
- **Shorten it.** Pick a smaller estimate. The note goes as soon as the task fits.
- **Pick another day.** Any day other than today is fine.

## Refuse or only warn

**Refuse work that doesn't fit** is on unless you turn it off. It is in the same place as your [day's hours](/features/day-shape), and it applies on both apps.

- **On**, **Done** or **Save** stays grey until the task fits.
- **Off**, you see the same note and the same **Move to**, but you can still save. The day goes over, and its reading says by how much, in orange.

Turn it off if you would rather record what a task will really take, even when the day can't hold it.

## What is never refused

The refusal only applies to saving a task on today that asks for more than today has.

- **Any other day.** A coming day never refuses, even one already over. [Upcoming](/features/upcoming-capacity) shows how it stands.
- **An edit that doesn't ask for more.** On a full day you can still rename a task that is already on today, shorten it, or change its tags. You see the note, and you can save.
- **A finished task.** Editing one is never refused.
- **Template tasks.** A [recurring](/features/recurring) task always lands on its day, full or not.
- **Calendar events.** They arrive whatever the day holds. Events from a feed set to [Treat as tasks](/features/events-that-count) then count toward the day.
- **Reopening or restoring a task.** It comes back to its day even if that puts the day over.
- **The Mac's menu bar panel.** Its quick add never throws away what you typed. The task is added, the panel says **Added, but it doesn't fit today**, and it offers **Move to**.

## Cases you may run into

- **Late in the day.** Today's room is measured from now, so it shrinks as the day goes on. Once **End of day** has passed there is no room left, and with refusing on, a new task can't go on today. **Move to** takes it to the next day with room.
- **A task longer than your focus cap.** An 8h task with a 6h cap fits no day. **Move to** offers tomorrow, which takes it because coming days never refuse. That day then reads as over.
- **No room for 60 days.** Matter looks 60 days ahead. If none of them has room, **Move to** offers tomorrow.
- **Work carried over.** Open tasks from earlier days count toward today, so they use up today's room. Finishing or pushing them gives it back.
- **Time already tracked.** Tracked time comes off what a task still needs, so a session in progress leaves more room. See [Sessions](/features/sessions).
